## Fidelity boundary

These are editable layout drafts, not pixel-identical approved reproductions. The demonstration hero and logo are separate runtime crops of decoded reference stills; uploaded campaign originals replace them. Original standalone artwork, transparent logo, patterned background and production font are absent. Decorative coins may be clipped at crop edges and logo crops can show background seams. Outfit approximates the font. The legal text remains editable campaign data; complete text is retained even where a reference clips it. These visible differences remain pending original assets and visual approval.
